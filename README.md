# 🏏 IPL Dashboard (2008–2018)

## 📘 Project Overview
This repository contains an **interactive Excel Dashboard** analyzing **Indian Premier League (IPL)** data from **2008–2018**.  
The project transforms raw match records into **visual insights** — helping cricket fans, analysts, and data enthusiasts uncover trends in team performance, toss decisions, player achievements, and venue statistics.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Explore IPL match outcomes across 11 seasons.  
- Compare **team performance** when batting vs fielding first.  
- Analyze **toss decisions** and their impact on match results.  
- Highlight **top venues** and their win distributions.  
- Showcase **top 10 Man of the Match (MOM)** award winners.  
- Summarize **season champions, runner-ups, and standout players**.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights
- **Season Summary:** Winners, runner-ups, Player of the Match & Series (2008–2018).  
- **Team Analysis:** Wins by batting first vs fielding first.  
- **Toss Decision Impact:** Winning % based on toss choice.  
- **Venue Insights:** Top 10 stadiums with match and win breakdowns.  
- **Player Performance:** MOM award leaders across seasons.  
- **Title Winners Overview:** Championship counts comparison.

---

## 🧠 Tech Stack
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - PivotTables & PivotCharts  
  - Power Pivot for data modeling  
  - Slicers & dynamic filters  
  - Conditional formatting for clarity

## 🚀 Insights
- Teams fielding first won **~70–80%** of matches in most seasons.  
- **Chennai Super Kings** and **Mumbai Indians** dominated multiple years.  
- **Shane Watson**, **Chris Gayle**, and **Sachin Tendulkar** stood out as consistent performers.  
- Venues like **Wankhede**, **Eden Gardens**, and **M. Chinnaswamy Stadium** hosted the most matches.

This dashboard demonstrates how **sports data visualization** can turn raw statistics into **strategic insights** — bridging the gap between analytics and fan engagement.
